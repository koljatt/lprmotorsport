# Driver hand sprite

This is the source reference for the active continuous arm asset, [`driver-arm.png`](driver-arm.png). The renderer now uses that complete glove-and-forearm cutout instead of joining this short cuff to a separate sleeve. See [`driver-arm.md`](driver-arm.md) for its prompt and rendering notes.

`driver-hand.png` was generated with the built-in image_gen tool using a transparent background. The user-provided cockpit photo was used as a viewpoint reference. The sprite depicts the driver's own right hand in a black and emerald-green fabric racing glove: smooth back of the hand toward the camera, thumb on the inner edge, curled fingers hidden beyond the wheel grip. The renderer mirrors it for the left hand and rotates both with the steering wheel. The sleeve cutout joins the separately rendered forearm.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent right-hand sprite for first-person Formula Student cockpit. The attached racing cockpit photo is a supporting reference for viewpoint and grip ONLY, not an edit target. Create exactly ONE RIGHT hand wearing a fitted BLACK and EMERALD GREEN fabric MOTORSPORT glove and short dark forest green suit sleeve, isolated with true alpha transparency. Critically match what a driver sees looking down at their OWN right hand gripping the wheel at 3 o'clock as in the reference: a broad smooth back of the glove facing the driver, a narrow thumb along the upper-left INSIDE edge of the invisible wheel grip, wrist entering from bottom slightly to right. The four fingers curl FORWARD away from camera around the far side of the wheel grip, and are HIDDEN from the driver's view. Do NOT depict a row of four protruding knuckles, individual curled finger tips, a sideways fist or a hand pointing sideways. No hard knuckle armor (this is an auto racing glove, not motorcycle glove), no prominent finger padding, no fingerless glove. Smooth glove back with subtle fabric shading, tasteful emerald green central panel and black edges, flexible cloth at wrist. Thumb is short and bent over the inner edge, not extended or sticking upright. Natural wrist-to-hand anatomy. Eye-level driver's perspective from slightly above and behind the wrist, NOT an external camera facing the driver's hand. The steering wheel itself must NOT be rendered; transparent empty space where wheel would be. Exactly one glove with sleeve cuff, occupying middle 70 percent of square image, entire silhouette within frame, rest transparent. Soft natural daylight. Photorealistic, no outlines, no text, branding or logos. The correct RIGHT hand has its thumb toward the LEFT (toward wheel centre); broad back of glove is on RIGHT, fingers disappear forward behind the unseen grip.
